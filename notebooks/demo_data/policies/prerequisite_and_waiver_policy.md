# Prerequisite, Corequisite, and Waiver Policy

## 1. Standard eligibility

A prerequisite is satisfied only when the official record shows a passing completed result or an approved
equivalency. A course currently in progress does not satisfy a prerequisite unless the catalog explicitly
allows concurrent registration. Corequisites must be taken in the same term unless previously completed.

## 2. Failed, withdrawn, and pending work

Failed and withdrawn courses do not satisfy prerequisites. Pending transfer credit does not satisfy a
prerequisite until the equivalency decision is posted. The system must clearly distinguish these statuses
from completed credit.

## 3. Waiver requests

A student may request a waiver when equivalent preparation can be documented through transfer work,
professional certification, research, or another approved course. The request must identify the blocked
course, each unmet prerequisite, supporting evidence, and the academic reason for the exception.

## 4. Approval boundary

Only the course-owning department or delegated academic advisor may approve a prerequisite waiver. The
planning agent may prepare and submit a request but must not treat the course as eligible before approval.

## 5. Specialization gateway courses

Gateway courses are not administrative formalities. Students pursuing a specialization should schedule
missing gateways early enough to avoid delaying advanced electives and capstone work. A waiver for one
course does not automatically satisfy specialization completion rules.

# Northstar Institute Course Registration Policy

## 1. Authoritative records

The student information system is the authoritative source for program, catalog year, completed courses,
grades, academic standing, active holds, and registered sections. Preferences remembered from advising
sessions may guide recommendations but may not replace official academic records.

## 2. Registration sequence

Students should review their degree audit before selecting courses. The registration service validates
prerequisites, duplicate credit, program restrictions, seat availability, timetable conflicts, and credit
limits when enrollment is submitted. A proposed plan does not guarantee a seat.

## 3. Seat reservations

A seat reservation is temporary and exists only to support an active registration attempt. A reservation
expires after twenty minutes unless it is committed through enrollment. Repeating a reservation request
with the same idempotency key must return the original reservation rather than consume another seat.

## 4. Timetable conflicts

Two sections conflict when any scheduled meeting overlaps. Students may not register for conflicting
sections unless the departments document an alternative participation arrangement and the academic advisor
approves the exception. Travel time and accessibility constraints should also be checked even when meeting
times do not strictly overlap.

## 5. Wait-lists

When no seat is available, a student may join one wait-list for that course. Joining a wait-list is not
enrollment and must not be presented as a confirmed seat. The student remains responsible for maintaining a
valid alternative plan until an offer is accepted.

## 6. Student confirmation

The planning assistant may create draft plans and check availability. It must not submit enrollment without
an explicit student confirmation or an authorized advisor action recorded for the same plan version.

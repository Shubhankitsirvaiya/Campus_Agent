# Degree Progress and Specialization Policy

## 1. Degree audit purpose

A degree audit compares completed, in-progress, and proposed work with the catalog requirements attached to
the student's admission cohort. Total earned credits are insufficient evidence of completion; required
courses, category minimums, capstone work, and minimum CGPA must each be satisfied.

## 2. Catalog year

Requirements normally follow the student's catalog year. A student may move to a newer catalog only through
a documented curriculum-transition decision. The planner must not silently apply requirements from another
cohort because course names appear similar.

## 3. Double counting

A course may satisfy only the categories allowed by the program rules. The same credits must not be counted
twice merely because a course appears relevant to two categories. Specialization electives may also count as
program electives only where the program configuration permits it.

## 4. Artificial Intelligence specialization

For the 2024 B.Tech. CSE catalog, Artificial Intelligence requires a minimum CGPA of 7.0, completion of
MA202 Linear Algebra for Computing and CS301 Machine Learning Foundations as gateway courses, and at least
18 approved specialization credits. Students missing a gateway should prioritize it before advanced AI
electives that depend on it.

## 5. Graduation forecasts

Graduation estimates are planning projections, not guarantees. They must state assumptions about future
course offerings, passing grades, capacity, internship completion, and approvals. A forecast should identify
requirements that create sequencing risk.

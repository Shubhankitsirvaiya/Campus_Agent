# Accessibility and Student Data Policy

## 1. Accessibility constraints

Approved accessibility requirements are mandatory planning constraints. A plan must not recommend an
inaccessible room as the only option. Where travel time between buildings is relevant, the planner should
preserve the recorded transition period or escalate the scheduling conflict.

## 2. Minimum disclosure

Tool results and audit logs should include only the student information necessary to support the current
academic task. Medical details, private advisor notes, and unrelated risk indicators must not be copied into
student-facing explanations.

## 3. Memory boundaries

Long-term memory may store scheduling preferences, academic goals, and previously approved planning
constraints. Official grades, academic standing, holds, and degree requirements must always be re-read from
authoritative systems instead of trusted from conversational memory.

## 4. Advisor review

When a safe timetable cannot be created under an approved accessibility requirement, the system should
create an advisor or accessibility-office review rather than ask the student to waive the requirement.
